## The operational problem

Safety teams needed a consistent way to review PPE and restricted-zone events in daily operations.

## How the workflow worked

The on-premises pilot used existing cameras to raise alerts for human review. Face recognition was not delivered. The pilot ran from 11 May to 3 July 2026 across 40 cameras.

## Measured results

- **PPE compliance**: 68.88 → 91.08 % (met; target >= 90%; source: `Project_Drishti_Library/04_delivery/pilot_acceptance_certificate_2026-07-06.md`)
- **Restricted-zone breach rate**: 5.58 → 3.0 breaches / 1,000 camera-hours (missed; target 50% reduction; source: `Project_Drishti_Library/04_delivery/pilot_acceptance_certificate_2026-07-06.md`)
- **False alarm rate**: not established → 9.68 % (met; target < 10%; source: `Project_Drishti_Library/04_delivery/pilot_acceptance_certificate_2026-07-06.md`)
- **Alert acknowledgement proxy**: not established → 4.9 minutes (missed; target <= 3 minutes; source: `Project_Drishti_Library/04_delivery/pilot_acceptance_certificate_2026-07-06.md`)

## Lessons and limitations

- The restricted-zone reduction target was missed.
- Acknowledgement remained above target and is reported as a proxy.
- Financial savings and causal safety outcomes are not established.
- Face recognition was removed for legal and privacy reasons.
