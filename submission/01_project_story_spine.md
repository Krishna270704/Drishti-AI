## Story summary
A manufacturer moved from an initial safety-monitoring enquiry to a scoped CCTV-based AI pilot. The signed BRD established camera-measured baselines and revised KPI targets. PPE compliance improved and restricted-zone breach rate fell, while acknowledgement time missed its target and restricted-zone reduction remained below target. Face recognition was removed after privacy/legal review. Potential financial value was discussed later, but attribution was not established. Publication remains blocked pending formal written approval.

## Guardrails
- Do not mix the 74% manual-walk baseline with the 68.9% camera baseline.
- Do not describe face recognition as delivered.
- Do not present vendor-draft claims as verified.
- Treat finance estimates as estimates, not project-attributable savings.
- Do not publish identifying details without written approval.
