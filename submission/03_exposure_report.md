# Exposure Report

**Contract basis:** `01_contract/MSA_confidentiality_and_publicity_extract.md`. Confidential categories include plant layouts, production capacity, process parameters, video images, incident records, commercial terms and site/supplier identities. Third-party disclosure requires prior written consent.

| Exposure | Risk | Safe replacement |
|---|---|---|
| Client, plant or unit name | Direct identification | “A large industrial manufacturer” |
| Site location / distinctive geography | Re-identification | “A multi-line manufacturing site” |
| Plant layout, camera locations, process parameters | Operational/security exposure | High-level workflow only |
| Employee names, faces, badges, video | Personal-data exposure | No faces, names, badges or raw footage |
| Incident dates/details | Identifies people or vulnerabilities | Approved aggregate trends only |
| Supplier names and commercial terms | Commercial confidentiality | “Technology implementation partner”; omit pricing |
| Production capacity / volumes | Sensitive operations | Omit unless approved |
| KPI figures combined with site/time details | Indirect identification | Approved rounded/aggregated figures |

## Controls
- Anonymise client and site by default.
- Exclude faces, badges, raw video, incident-level details and precise site references.
- Keep confidential source material local; do not upload it to public services.
- Retain an internal evidence trail separately from external copy.
- Obtain written approval from authorised Legal and Corporate Communications representatives.
- WhatsApp is not a substitute for formal written permission.
- If uncertain, exclude and escalate.
