# Three Case Study Versions

> Anonymised drafts only. External use is blocked pending written client Legal and Corporate Communications approval.

## Gatekeeper – Risk, compliance and control
### Making safety monitoring more measurable with existing CCTV
A large industrial manufacturer piloted AI-assisted video analytics using its existing CCTV environment, focusing on PPE compliance and restricted-zone monitoring. During the camera-measured pilot, PPE compliance increased from 68.9% to 91.1%, a 22.2 percentage-point improvement. Restricted-zone breach rate decreased from 5.58 to 3.00 per 1,000 camera hours, a 46% reduction. False alarms were 9.7% in the final two-week window.

The pilot also exposed areas for improvement: restricted-zone reduction was below the 50% target and the 4.9-minute acknowledgement proxy was above the ≤3-minute target. Face recognition was removed following privacy/legal review. The results support measured safety workflows, not autonomous safety management.

## User – Day-to-day workflow
### Helping safety teams prioritise alerts
A manufacturer piloted AI-assisted analysis of existing CCTV to help its safety team identify PPE and restricted-zone events and route alerts into its operational workflow. Camera-measured PPE compliance moved from 68.9% to 91.1%; restricted-zone breaches per 1,000 camera hours fell from 5.58 to 3.00.

The pilot did not remove the need for people. Acknowledgement time remained 4.9 minutes against a target of no more than 3 minutes. The next improvement cycle should focus on alert handling, operational adoption and continued measurement.

## Buyer – Business outcome
### A measurable pilot with clear next-step economics
A large industrial manufacturer evaluated an AI-enabled safety-monitoring workflow built around existing CCTV. PPE compliance improved by 22.2 percentage points, while restricted-zone breach rate fell by 46%. False alarms were 9.7% in the final two-week window.

Two targets remained open: restricted-zone reduction (46% against 50%) and acknowledgement time (4.9 minutes against ≤3 minutes). A later finance discussion identified a possible gate-detention opportunity, but other operational changes may have contributed. No project-attributable ROI is claimed.
